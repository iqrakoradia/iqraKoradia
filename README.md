## 👋 Who am I?

I’m **Iqra Koradia**—Founder at **Will & Way**, a data analyst, and a product-minded builder based in Mumbai. I love taking a half-formed idea or a messy brief and giving it shape: a clear product direction, thoughtful experience, and working technology people can actually use.

My work sits where **creative technology, business intelligence, and human-centered products** meet. I’m especially curious about what happens when good data and good design work together.

## 💡 My vision

> **Ideas, made possible.**

I believe promising ideas deserve more than a slide deck. My vision is to bridge imagination and execution—bringing strategy, design, engineering, and data together to make digital products useful, thoughtful, and ready for the real world.

## 🚀 What I build

### Founder · Will & Way

**Will & Way · Full-time** · Sep 2026–Present · Mumbai, Maharashtra, India · Remote

I co-founded a global creative technology studio with a simple promise: **turn ideas into market-ready digital products**. We build full-stack web platforms, mobile applications, admin panels, and data-driven business tools for startups and growing companies.

- Lead end-to-end execution—from early concepts and product roadmaps to production-ready **React, Next.js, and Node.js** applications.
- Bring together UI/UX integration, database architecture, API engineering, and client strategy.
- Partner with founders on performance improvements and white-label product delivery.
- Explore the [Will & Way portfolio](https://willandwayportfolio.framer.website/) to see the studio’s approach and work.
